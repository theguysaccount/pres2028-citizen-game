# Product

**User.** Incremental-game players (the *Universal Paperclips* and *Cookie Clicker* crowd). Also politically curious teenagers and adults who want to understand the electoral college and field organizing, and teachers looking for a playable explainer.

**Painful job.** "Show me how 270 actually works, in something I would play rather than read." Today that means a dry civics explainer, or an idle game with no ideas in it.

**Promise.** One conversation at a time, build a citizen campaign with no party and no candidate, and win 270 real electoral votes before Election Day. About 20–35 minutes, on a phone or a laptop.

**Wedge.**
- The result card ("I won the 2028 race on day 198"), plus a `?day=` link that dares a friend to win sooner.
- A guide that answers "electoral votes by state" and "how many states to win 270" from the game's real table.
- Embeddable for classrooms.

**Why now.** The 2028 cycle is starting. In November 2026 the game's 730-day clock is roughly the real time left before Election Day 2028.

**Activation metric.** `campaign_won`. The swing factor is shares per win. Both count once the GoatCounter key is set.

**First revenue event.** A pay-what-you-want payment on an itch.io page, through the footer support link (OPERATIONS.md, keys 2 and 4). Never a donation to a campaign.

**What v1 deliberately won't do.**
- No real party, candidate, campaign, slogan or link to one.
- No donations, sign-ups, volunteer capture or voter contact.
- No accounts, leaderboard, ads or cookies.
- No mandates that mirror a real platform.
